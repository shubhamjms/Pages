The Builder's Fieldnotes is a public notebook for practical learning: concise explanations, complete guides, and tools that earn their place through use.

## What belongs here

Material should help a reader understand a system, make a decision, or build something they can inspect.

## Colophon

The site is built from Markdown, generated with Vite, and published as a static application on GitHub Pages.
